Okay, let's slow down, because the feeds are doing that thing again where they scream 'SHOCKING' about a number that was never a secret.

The Galactic Central Bank published its quarterly Credit Dilution Index yesterday. 9.2%, annualized, Core Systems basket. ENN cut to a trader on Ceres Exchange clutching his dataslate like it personally betrayed him. Panic selling on freight futures. Prime lending rate speculation already looping on every mobi device from here to the Kardashia Corridor.

The 9.2% number isn't news. It's an invoice — arriving eleven weeks after the actual event. The event was the GCB's last liquidity injection into its Primary Dilution Partners: Stellar Financial, Orion Trust, and the three shipyard consortiums that exist almost entirely to absorb freshly minted credits before anyone else even knows they've been minted.

Follow the sequence:

**Week 1** — GCB issues new credit allocation "to stabilize interstellar lending." Goes straight to the Partners.
**Week 2–4** — Partners buy what's actually scarce: shipyard real estate, asteroid claims, long-haul freight contracts. Prices on those assets move immediately. Nobody outside the terminal sees it.
**Week 5–10** — Credit trickles through corporate payrolls, subcontractor invoices, finally into colony administration budgets.
**Week 11** — You get your wages. Prices at the frontier commissary have already adjusted. The GCB then publishes a dilution index measuring the gap between when you got paid and when prices moved — and calls it a surprise.

That gap has a name. Three hundred years ago on Earth they called it the Cantillon Effect and didn't bother hiding it, because nobody outside a trading floor knew the term. Now we've got a galaxy's worth of financial media whose entire business model is keeping it that way — dressing the invoice up as a plot twist every single quarter.

And right on schedule, somebody's selling you the fix. A broadcaster calling himself the Insurgent Capitalist is running a subscription holoreel — "Rebel Capitalist Pro," 40 credits a cycle — promising the cheat code to protect your wealth from exactly the mechanism his own sponsors' Partners are running upstream of you. That's not rebellion. That's a toll booth on the road out of the trap, operated by a man who knows precisely where the trap is because he's standing next to it.

The actual cheat code was never proprietary. It's sequencing. Ask who received the credit first, not what the index says eleven weeks later. The index is the receipt. The receipt is not the crime.

No subscription required. Just a willingness to look upstream before you look at the headline.
