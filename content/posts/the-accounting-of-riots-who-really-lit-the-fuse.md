Okay okay okay, let me draw this out for you—

ENN keeps running the same segment: masked dockworkers torching a Colony Administration kiosk, a somber anchor intoning about 'fractured legitimacy' and 'rising extremism.' Every panel agrees something is broken. Nobody on the panel says what.

So let's build it from the ground up.

**Step one: the old consensus.** Three centuries back, the Earth Unified Council operated on a genuinely boring premise — do almost nothing, tax almost nothing, print nothing. That consensus didn't erode on its own. It got dynamited, piece by piece, by Expansion-Era administrators who discovered that 'peacekeeping operations' and settlement subsidies were a lot easier to fund with a printing terminal than with an honest ledger.

**Step two: enter the Galactic Central Bank.** The GCB doesn't counterfeit credits in your pocket — it issues them upstream, to mega-corps and fleet contractors, who spend them *before* prices adjust. By the time the new credits trickle down to a dockhand on Kepler Freight, every price on the station has already moved. That's not inflation hitting everyone equally. That's a wealth pump, running one direction, and it's been running for three hundred years.

Follow the pump far enough and you get Side-Effect Number One: real wages flatline while asset holders — the people who already owned ships, shares, station leases — get richer just for holding things. Side-Effect Number Two: the top 1% of Core Systems households now controls roughly 32% of total system wealth. Side-Effect Number Three: the bond pits, once the galaxy's shock absorber, stopped absorbing anything, because the GCB has spent two decades leaning on the yield curve to keep its own deficit cheap. When that grip finally slipped last cycle, the long-dated notes cleared 5% and every retirement fund built on the old 'safe mix' took a body blow nobody warned them about.

Now watch a Council candidate go on the feeds and promise that credit dilution will 'pay off the debt.' He's not wrong, technically. Debasement does erase debt. It erases it by erasing your savings first.

The riot isn't the disease. It's the symptom finally visible to the naked eye. Three centuries ago an actual commission was convened, studied a return to metal-backed credit, tipped its ceremonial hat to the idea, and buried the report. We're still living in the crater that decision left.

You want fewer burning kiosks? Stop treating 'legitimacy' as a feelings problem. Audit the pump. Financial literacy isn't a hobby anymore — on a station with a currency terminal in every corridor, it's self-defense.

That's the whole scam, drawn out on one board. Took three hundred years to build. Took me about four minutes to show you.
